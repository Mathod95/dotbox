What Homarr does

  • It shows your apps on boards you arrange yourself, by drag and drop, with no file to write.
  • Its widgets read the apps through their API: the streams of Jellyfin or Plex, the requests of Seerr (approve or decline them from the board), the downloads of qBittorrent, the calendar of Radarr and Sonarr.
  • It has its own accounts, groups and permissions, to share a board with others.

Dotbox manages the server, Homarr shows your apps: its web interface stays for Dotbox itself.

What the install does

  • It creates /opt/homarr/config, owned by your user.
  • It draws Homarr's encryption key once, 64 hexadecimal characters, in /opt/dotbox/secrets/homarr_encryption_key, readable by root only. Homarr reads it from a file: it never appears in the compose.
  • It writes /opt/homarr/compose.yml, starts Homarr behind Authelia and waits for it to answer.

Where the files are

On the serverIn the containerWhat it is
/opt/homarr/compose.ymlGenerated by dotbox, rewritten on every install: do not edit it.
/opt/homarr/compose.override.ymlYours, optional: your changes to the compose, never touched by dotbox.
/opt/homarr/config/db//appdata/db/Its database: boards, users, integrations.
/opt/homarr/config/redis//appdata/redis/Its cache.
/opt/dotbox/secrets/homarr_encryption_key/run/secrets/encryption_keyThe key that encrypts the credentials of the integrations. Lose it and they must be entered again: it is saved with the dotbox backup.

What Dotbox sets

SettingValueWhy
PUID, PGIDyour userThe image starts as root, gives /appdata to your user, then runs Homarr as your user.
SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY_FILE/run/secrets/encryption_keyThe key, read from the file Dotbox mounts read-only.
Docker socketnot mountedHomarr does not manage containers: Dotbox does.

In the compose: no published port, no-new-privileges and an automatic restart. The healthcheck is the image's own.

After the install

Open its address: Authelia, then Homarr's first-run setup, which creates its admin account. Then add your apps as integrations, by their container name: http://jellyfin:8096, http://seerr:5055, http://radarr:7878, http://qbittorrent:8080. Their API keys stay encrypted in Homarr's database.
